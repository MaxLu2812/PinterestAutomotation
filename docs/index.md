# Pinterest Automotation — Personal Pinterest Management Tool

A personal utility for organizing and scheduling Pinterest content. This tool helps individual users manage their Pinterest boards, organize pins, and plan their content calendar efficiently.

## What It Does

- **Content Organization** — Structure and categorize your Pinterest boards and pins
- **Pin Scheduling** — Plan when your content appears on Pinterest
- **Board Management** — Organize boards by theme, aesthetic, or category
- **Personal Use Only** — Designed for individual content creators and hobbyists

## Key Principles

- **Manual Review** — All content is reviewed before publication
- **No Third-Party Data** — Your content stays under your control
- **Personal Tool** — Not a service, not a platform, not a business
