# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** June 23, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Pinterest Automotation ("the tool," "we," or "us") handles information when you use our personal Pinterest management application. By using this tool, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

## 2. Information We Access

### Pinterest Account Information
The tool accesses your Pinterest account exclusively through the official Pinterest API v5. With your authorization, it may access:
- Your public profile information
- Your boards and their contents
- Your pins and their metadata

### Locally Stored Information
The following data is stored exclusively on your local machine:
- Pinterest API access tokens (encrypted in environment variables)
- Content drafts and schedules
- Configuration preferences
- Generated metadata for content organization

## 3. How We Use Information

The tool uses accessed information solely for:
- Displaying your boards and content within the application
- Organizing and categorizing your Pinterest content
- Scheduling content publication according to your preferences
- Managing your boards and pins as directed by you

## 4. Data Storage and Security

- **API Tokens**: Stored as local environment variables on your machine
- **Content Data**: Stored in a local SQLite database
- **No Cloud Storage**: All data remains on your local system
- **No External Servers**: The tool communicates only with Pinterest's official API

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not:
- Sell, trade, or rent your personal information
- Share your data with third parties
- Use your data for advertising or analytics
- Collect telemetry or usage statistics
- Store data on external servers

The only external communication is with Pinterest's official API v5 endpoints, and only when you explicitly perform actions such as publishing or fetching content.

## 6. User Control

You have full control over your data:
- All locally stored data can be deleted at any time
- API tokens can be revoked from your Pinterest Developer settings
- You choose what content to publish and when
- The tool does not automate without your explicit instruction

## 7. Third-Party Services

This tool communicates with the following third-party services only when explicitly triggered by your actions:
- **Pinterest API v5** — For authorized board and pin management

Each service has its own privacy policy governing data handling.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in this document with an updated date.

## 9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository or contact the repository owner directly through GitHub.
